EST. 2014 OUR STORY

Crafting Excellence Since 2014

VELORA was founded by Alexandra Chen in 2014, born from a singular conviction: that luxury interior design should be more than aesthetic perfection — it should resonate deeply with how people live, work, and experience the world.

What began as a small boutique studio in New York City has grown into one of the world's most respected design practices, with studios in Milan, Dubai, Singapore, and London. Our portfolio spans private residences, flagship hotels, Michelin-starred restaurants, iconic corporate campuses, and premier retail destinations.

Every space we design is a conversation between art and life — where timeless beauty meets purposeful function.

— Alexandra Chen, Founder & Principal Designer

We approach every commission as a unique narrative — one that begins with listening deeply to our clients and ends with a space that exceeds every expectation, inspiring daily life for generations to come.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Core Values

Uncompromising Quality

We accept nothing short of excellence in every material, detail, and execution. Our standards are defined by the world's finest craftspeople and most discerning clients.

Client Partnership

Every project begins with deep listening. We treat each client relationship as a true partnership — building trust, transparency, and a shared commitment to the final vision.

Creative Innovation

We constantly push the boundaries of what luxury design can be — embracing new materials, technologies, and perspectives while honouring the timeless principles of great design.

Sustainable Luxury

We believe the most enduring luxury is responsible luxury. Our designs prioritise longevity, ethical sourcing, and environmental consciousness without compromise.

"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it lives with you — day after day, year after year, becoming more beautiful as it becomes more yours."
— VELORA DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
OUR JOURNEY

A Decade of Excellence

2014

VELORA Founded

Alexandra Chen establishes VELORA Design Studio in New York City with a team of 5 designers and a singular vision for luxury interior excellence.

2016

First International Award

VELORA receives the IIDA Design Award for Innovation in Workplace Design, placing us on the global stage and attracting clients from Europe and the Middle East.

2018

Global Expansion

Studios open in Milan and Dubai, expanding our capacity to serve high-net-worth clients across Europe and the Middle East. Team grows to 20 designers.

2021

Asia Pacific Launch

Singapore and Hong Kong studios launch under Yuki Tanaka's leadership. The Grand Velora Hotel project establishes VELORA as Asia's leading luxury design firm.

2025

18 Awards, 250+ Projects

VELORA celebrates its most awarded year — winning Best Luxury Interior Design 2025, Best Hotel Interior Asia, and the prestigious Residential Design of the Year from Architectural Digest.
