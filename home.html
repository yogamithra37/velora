ABOUT VELORA

We Design Extraordinary Spaces

VELORA is a world-renowned luxury interior design studio with a legacy of crafting spaces that are both breathtakingly beautiful and deeply functional. Founded over a decade ago, we have established ourselves as the definitive choice for discerning clients who demand nothing but the finest.

Our multi-disciplinary team of 30+ award-winning designers, architects, and artisans brings a uniquely holistic perspective to every project — from intimate residential retreats to grand hospitality landmarks across five continents.

The VELORA Difference

01

Unmatched Craftsmanship

Every project is treated as a masterwork. We collaborate exclusively with the finest artisans, material suppliers, and contractors — accepting nothing short of perfection at every stage of delivery.

Bespoke Design Philosophy

We believe no two clients share the same vision. Our design process begins with deep listening — understanding your lifestyle, aspirations, and aesthetic sensibilities before a single concept is sketched.
