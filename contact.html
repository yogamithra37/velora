QUICK ANSWERS

Before You Write

How quickly will you respond to my enquiry?

We personally review and respond to every enquiry within one business day. For urgent project requirements, call our New York studio directly — we maintain a dedicated new business line staffed during business hours.

Is the initial consultation free of charge?

Yes. Our initial discovery consultation — typically 45–60 minutes conducted in-person or via video call — is completely complimentary for all projects over 1,000 sq ft. This conversation helps us understand your vision and allows us to explain how VELORA can bring it to life.

Do I need a fully formed brief before contacting you?

Absolutely not. Many of our most successful projects have begun with a client who simply knew their space could be extraordinary but wasn't sure how to articulate what they wanted. Our discovery process is specifically designed to draw out your vision — even when it's still forming. Come as you are.

Can I visit one of your studios in person?

We welcome studio visits by appointment at all five of our locations. Our showrooms feature material libraries, project photography, and sample pieces from our custom furniture collections. Contact your nearest studio to arrange a visit with a senior designer.

What information should I prepare before the consultation?

It is helpful to gather floor plans of your space (if available), a rough indication of your budget range, and any images of spaces, materials, or art that inspire you — Pinterest boards and magazine tearsheets are perfect. Most importantly, think about how you use the space and how you want to feel when you're in it. That emotional brief is often more valuable than technical specifications.

Still have questions?

Call us directly or send a message — our team is always happy to help.
