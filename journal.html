Key Trends of 2026

01

Neo-Maximalism

After years of minimal restraint, luxury interiors are embracing bold pattern mixing, sculptural furniture, and richly layered palettes that celebrate visual abundance.

Trending Now
02

Artisanal Craftsmanship

Clients are moving away from mass production toward hand-crafted, one-of-a-kind pieces. Visible craft, natural materials, and maker stories are luxury's new status markers.

Rising Fast

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical luxury interior design project take?
Project timelines vary significantly based on scope. A single-room redesign typically takes 8–12 weeks from concept approval to completion. A full residential project averages 6–12 months. Large-scale hospitality or corporate projects range from 12–24 months. We provide a detailed timeline at the outset of every project and maintain rigorous schedule management throughout.
What is the minimum project size VELORA accepts?
We take on projects of all sizes — from single bespoke rooms to 300,000 sq ft hospitality landmarks. Our Studio tier is designed for targeted single-room projects, while our Signature and Prestige tiers serve full residential, commercial, and hospitality commissions. We believe every client deserves access to exceptional design regardless of scale.
Do you work internationally, and how do you manage projects across borders?
Yes — VELORA operates globally with studios in New York, Milan, Dubai, Singapore, and London. We have completed projects in over 40 countries. For international projects, we assign a lead designer from the nearest regional studio and leverage our global network of trusted contractors and suppliers. Regular video calls, digital collaboration platforms, and on-site visits keep every project on track.
How do you handle budget management and cost control?
Transparency is fundamental to how we work. We establish a detailed project budget at the outset and review it at every stage gate. Our procurement team leverages longstanding supplier relationships to secure competitive pricing without compromising quality. We provide monthly budget reports and alert you immediately to any potential variations — we never surprise our clients with cost overruns.
What is your design fee structure?
Our fees are structured based on the scope and complexity of each commission. For most residential and commercial projects we charge a flat design fee agreed upfront, supplemented by a procurement management fee for FF&E purchasing. We do not take supplier commissions, ensuring completely unbiased specification. All fees are discussed transparently during your initial consultation.
Can I see 3D visualisations before any construction begins?
Absolutely. Photorealistic 3D visualisations are standard in our Signature and Prestige tiers. You will be able to virtually walk through your completed space and see exactly how every material, finish, and furniture piece will look in context — all before a single wall is touched. Prestige clients also receive an immersive VR walkthrough experience.
What happens if I want to make changes once construction has begun?
We understand that design is an evolving process. Minor specification changes during construction are handled at no additional design fee. Significant scope changes — such as altering the spatial layout or adding new rooms — are assessed individually. Our project manager will provide a clear impact assessment showing schedule and cost implications before any change is approved.
Do you offer post-project maintenance or styling services?
Yes. All VELORA projects include a 12-month post-completion support period covering minor adjustments, furniture repositioning, and seasonal styling changes. Our Prestige clients benefit from a comprehensive two-year aftercare programme including annual design reviews, priority contractor access, and a dedicated account manager for any requirement that arises.
