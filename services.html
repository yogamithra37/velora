HOW WE WORK

Our Design Process

A proven six-phase process refined over 12 years and 250+ projects to ensure extraordinary results every time.

PHASE 01

Discovery & Vision

We begin with in-depth conversations about your vision, lifestyle, aspirations, and functional requirements. This phase includes site surveys, precedent research, and the development of a detailed project brief that becomes our guiding document throughout design.

PHASE 02

Concept Development

Our design team develops two or three distinct concept directions — each supported by mood boards, material samples, and spatial sketches. We present these concepts with full explanations of how each addresses your brief, then refine the chosen direction until it feels exactly right.

PHASE 03

Design Development

The approved concept is developed into comprehensive design drawings, photorealistic 3D visualisations, and detailed material and furniture specifications. You will see exactly how your space will look and feel before a single wall is touched.

PHASE 04

Technical Documentation

Our team produces construction-ready technical drawings, electrical and lighting layouts, furniture schedules, and a comprehensive specification package. This documentation is the blueprint that ensures every contractor delivers exactly what was designed.

PHASE 05

Project Execution

VELORA manages procurement, contractor coordination, site supervision, and quality control throughout construction. Your dedicated project manager provides weekly progress reports and is always available to resolve issues swiftly so delivery remains on time and on budget.

PHASE 06

The Reveal

The final phase is our favourite — the complete styling of your space, art placement, accessory curation, and the joyous reveal. We document every completed space with professional photography and remain on hand for any post-completion refinements.
